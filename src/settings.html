What is Damu?

Damu is a small animated character who walks onto your screen every so often, tells you something with a voice clip and a speech bubble, and waits for you to press OK. Then he does something fun: cheers or runs. You can click, double-click and drag him too, and he reacts.

Good to know

  • Damu sits on top of your windows but never blocks them: clicks pass straight through everywhere except on Damu himself.
  • Find him in the system tray (Windows) or menu bar (macOS) to open Settings, pause dialogues or quit.
  • Closing this window doesn't quit Damu. Use Quit Damu from the tray or menu bar.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version: Damu works entirely on your computer. It collects nothing, sends nothing and has no accounts.

Data we collect

None. Damu has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or user accounts. The author never receives any information about you or how you use the app.

Data stored on your device

Damu saves your preferences (the options in this window) in a single file, settings.json, in the app's data folder:

It contains only settings such as visit interval, speed, size and audio options. Delete the file or use Reset to clear it. Uninstalling Damu removes the program; you can delete the data folder to remove the settings too.

Network access

Damu makes no network connections. All images, animations, fonts and voice clips are bundled with the app and loaded from your disk. There is no auto-update check. Buttons that open a web page (such as Buy me a coffee) just launch your default browser; Damu itself still makes no connections.

What Damu can see

  • Damu draws a transparent window over your screen. It only reacts to your mouse pointer while it is over Damu himself (to let you click, drag and hover over him, and to make him glance toward the pointer). Pointer position is used on the spot and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Damu does not take screenshots, record the screen, log keystrokes, read the clipboard, or access your files, other windows, camera or microphone.

Start at login

If you turn this on, Damu registers itself as a sign-in item for your user account (a Windows startup entry or a macOS login item) so it starts when you log in. Turning it off removes that entry.

Third parties and children

No data is shared with any third party, because none is collected. Since Damu collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how Damu handles data, this page will be updated in that release and the date above will change.

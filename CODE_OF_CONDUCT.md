# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Damu's community to be friendly, welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Being kind, patient and respectful
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information without permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
issues and other contributions that don't follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour they consider harmful.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project
in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@abidkakkur11](https://github.com/abidkakkur11), by
opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/abidkakkur11/damu-companion/security/advisories/new)
(it is private to the maintainers, whatever the topic). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
